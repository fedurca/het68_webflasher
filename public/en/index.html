het68 firmware and hardware

Open-source project github.com/fedurca/het68 — a 6-channel I2S USB sound card and spatial acoustics platform.

What the firmware does

The het68 device becomes a 6-channel USB microphone (UAC2). It captures six independent I2S lines from ICS-43434 MEMS mics, moves samples with DMA, and streams them to the host via TinyUSB.

  • USB Audio Class 2.0 · isochronous IN
  • 6 channels · 48 kHz · 24-bit packed (S24_3LE)
  • PIO + DMA capture, pipeline for DOA / detection
  • Signed firmware images · deployed via the web flasher
