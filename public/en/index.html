About the project

An early welcome for unexpected visitors

Optical cameras need line of sight. Radars struggle with terrain shadow and electronic jamming. But a drone, an approaching train, or a defective axle share one thing — they make noise.

Project HET68 turns that noise into a tactical advantage. We built a compact, highly sensitive spatial-acoustics platform that analyses the sound field in real time. Nothing is shipped to a slow remote cloud. All the maths runs on the edge. In air defence and critical infrastructure the rule is simple: react late, and you are only clearing debris.

How it works

Maths instead of magic

More ears hear more

A microphone array senses the surroundings so even tiny time offsets are caught as the wavefront reaches each sensor.

Geometry instead of guessing

Direction-of-arrival (DOA) algorithms compute the angle and direction of the sound. Multiple nodes enable spatial localisation of a target.

No wasted chatter

Raw data is processed on the device. Out go only structured facts — direction and coordinates for systems that handle a suitably warm reception.

Open code, closed skies

No black-box gadgets with mystery insides. Our hardware and firmware are open-source. You know exactly what the device does, what data it processes, and how to integrate it into your existing security stack.

Memory etched into silicon.

The acronym HET68 (not 1968) is not just a code name for us. It is a reminder. In our history, a surprise from the sky once cost decades of freedom. We develop and deliver this technology with a clear aim: that no unexpected parade of foreign machines passes unnoticed — or without an immediate, precise response.

ISO/IEC 27001 · Secure by design

Built for upcoming ISO 27k certification

The HET68 platform and cloud service are designed in line with upcoming ISO/IEC 27001 certification and secure by design — security is part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

Secure by design

TLS, auth on every mutating call, least-privilege API keys, and separation of edge / gateway / console.

Audit & change control

Audit trail for calibration, sync, and settings — supporting controls expected by ISO 27k.

Minimal retention

Raw audio only when needed (companion HW). Detections and metadata take priority over long-term recordings.
